      “Shiftvert” Not Opened

      Apple could not verify “Shiftvert” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

      Open it once

      Double-click Shiftvert in Applications. macOS says it couldn't check Shiftvert for malware. That's normal for apps outside Apple's paid developer program: click Done (or OK), not Move to Trash.

      The wording varies by macOS version.

      Security

      “Shiftvert” was blocked to protect your Mac.

      Click Open Anyway

      Open Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and click Open Anyway next to “Shiftvert” was blocked. Confirm with Touch ID or your password; if macOS asks once more, click Open Anyway again.

      No Open Anyway button? It only shows for about an hour after you try to open the app, so double-click Shiftvert again and come back.

      Drag, then hold ⇧

      Look for in the menu bar, then start dragging any file and hold ⇧ Shift.

      Tip: pick the file up first, then press ⇧. Holding ⇧ before you click makes Finder select extra files.

      Can't see the icon (say, behind the notch)? Open Shiftvert again: its menu appears at the pointer. macOS may ask once to let it use your Desktop, Documents or Downloads folder so it can save results next to your files.
